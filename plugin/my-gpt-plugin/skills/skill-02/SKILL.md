# Skill 02 — Research and Knowledge

## Purpose
Research a domain using reliable source material.

## Procedure
1. Identify the exact question and required freshness.
2. Prefer official documentation and primary sources.
3. Cross-check important claims.
4. Record source URLs, dates, and limitations.
5. Summarize findings without overstating certainty.

## Output
A source-backed research note with findings, caveats, and references.
